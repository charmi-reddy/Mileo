Commute Hours

Mobile-first commute micro-learning for a real daily problem.

Unique Kernel

A commute-aware micro-learning app that respects real travel time and treats study as optional, bounded, and low-pressure.

Who It's For

Students and interns commuting by public transport, using a phone in a passenger-safe, non-driving context.

Core Loop

Set route + time → choose session → study a Python flashcard deck → confirm actual minutes → see totals.

Why This Matters

The learner has a real daily problem: a large block of commuting time gets lost. The app offers a meaningful but intentionally small improvement without turning the journey into a demand or a guilt spiral. Rest is always valid.

What "Working" Looks Like

A user can open the app, enter a route nickname and commute length, select a 5/10/15-minute session, finish a short flashcard run, confirm real study minutes, and have the dashboard show this week's “hours reclaimed” with a daily bar chart after refresh. Invalid input and zero-session states show clear messages instead of saving.

In Scope

  • One local user, no login
  • Route nickname and one-way commute duration
  • 5, 10, and 15 minute session lengths
  • One bundled Python interview-prep deck with 10 flashcards
  • Reveal-answer flow, understood/review-again tracking, and save-on-confirmation
  • Weekly summary as “hours reclaimed” with a bar chart by day
  • Refresh persistence, duplicate prevention, invalid-input handling, and edit/delete flows

Later

  • Offline queued saves and sync
  • Additional decks or optional audio
  • Improved analytics and richer UI polish
  • More route and preference management

Explicitly Cut

  • Accounts and social features
  • GPS, maps, route optimization, transit fares
  • Live AI generation, podcasts, or audio
  • Reminders, leaderboards, and multiple topics
  • Offline support before the core flow is validated
The project stays intentionally small: one user, one topic, one focused flow, and a proof that the app works on a narrow phone screen without pressuring the user.
